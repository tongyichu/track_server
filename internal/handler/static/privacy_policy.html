1. 我们处理哪些信息

1.1 账号与个人资料

使用手机号登录时，我们处理您填写的手机号、验证码及验证结果，以创建或识别账号。使用已开放的微信登录时，处理授权码、微信账号标识及您授权提供的昵称、头像。第三方登录以授权页展示的信息和您作出的授权为准。您可选填或修改昵称、头像等资料。

为维持登录、识别请求及保障账号安全，我们处理账号 ID、登录令牌、Android ID 等设备标识、IP 地址、客户端版本、系统平台和语言。不会要求您授权读取短信内容来填写验证码。

1.2 地图、定位与轨迹记录

在您使用定位、轨迹录制或同行位置服务时，相关功能处理经纬度、定位时间、精度、海拔、速度、方向等定位数据；地图和定位服务还可能在授权范围内处理网络、Wi-Fi 或基站信息以辅助定位。步数与运动识别信息用于运动统计和轨迹质量判断。

我们据此生成路线、起终点、城市或地址、运动类型、时间、距离、时长、配速、爬升和估算卡路里等记录，并可能做漂移过滤、交通状态识别和轨迹补偿。补偿点和运动估算可能存在误差。

没有位置数据便无法提供实际位置录制和实时位置共享。开启这些功能前应向您单独说明敏感信息处理的目的、必要性、方式及影响，并依法取得单独同意；操作系统的位置权限不能替代该同意。拒绝位置权限会影响对应功能，不影响与位置无关且已开放的功能。

1.3 本地保存、云端同步与投稿

轨迹点及相关记录保存在设备本地。当前完成保存后，App 会尝试向服务端创建轨迹摘要，包括标题、运动类型、城市或起点地址、起止时间及运动统计；网络失败可能导致云端与本地暂不一致。

当您使用上传、同步或投稿功能时，服务端还会处理所上传的原始轨迹文件、地图截图、打点内容、路线说明及您选取的图片，用于云端展示、路线发现、投稿审核、成就计算等对应服务。导入文件可能包含其他人的位置或图片，请确保有权使用。

1.4 同行、关注与路线发现

创建或加入同行房间时，我们处理房间 ID、成员账号、角色、加入或离开时间、在线状态、实时位置、速度、方向及会话内文字弹幕和关键事件，以展示成员状态、连接通信并形成同行记录。关注、粉丝、收藏和路线使用记录用于维护关系、收藏列表及路线服务。

路线推荐可能使用所选城市、运动类型、浏览、收藏及使用行为调整候选和排序。当前个性化推荐的关闭或非个性化入口为 {非个性化推荐入口}；正式开放个性化服务前须提供可用的选择方式。

1.5 反馈、诊断与使用统计

提交意见反馈时，我们处理您填写的内容、联系方式（如填写）及主动选取的图片。诊断信息可能涉及设备型号、系统与 App 版本、网络状态、错误信息和相关操作记录；上传前请避免包含验证码、令牌或不必要的位置细节。

App 使用自建统计接口处理安装标识、会话标识、登录用户 ID（如已登录）、页面浏览、功能点击、录制及上传结果、城市代码、设备和网络信息，用于功能分析、推荐效果评估和故障定位。去标识化统计仍可能属于个人信息，不等同于匿名信息。事件属性设置了手机号、令牌、验证码、原始经纬度和轨迹点等内容的脱敏规则；业务录制与同行定位另按前述条款处理。统计授权及关闭入口为 {使用统计授权及关闭入口}。

3. 轨迹与同行的可见范围

本地记录与云端记录的可见范围不同。当前普通云端轨迹按可被其他登录用户浏览、搜索或推荐的状态创建，不能把“登录后才能访问”理解为“仅自己可见”。完成录制后的自动摘要创建、后续原始文件上传和对外展示，应分别在对应流程清楚告知并取得依法所需的同意。正式公开流程及隐私控制入口：{轨迹公开确认及隐私控制入口}。

公开资料可能包括昵称、头像、公开成就、关注和粉丝关系及公开轨迹内容；手机号和登录凭证不属于公开主页内容。路线投稿可能被审核人员查看，并在获批后用于推荐或路线展示。撤回投稿不等于删除原轨迹。

参与同行会使房间内其他成员获得您的位置及同行状态；房间邀请链接、口令或二维码的转发可能扩大加入范围。房主及成员可能保存截图或行程记录。退出房间停止后续位置共享，但不会自动撤销其他成员已经看到或保存的内容；服务端既有会话、位置快照和事件的清理规则见保存期限。

使用系统分享、导出或发送截图时，您选择的接收方可取得文件内容。请避免将住所、未成年人活动地点或他人的敏感信息分享到不可信的渠道。

1. Information we handle

1.1 Accounts and profiles

Phone sign-in uses the number and verification code you enter and the verification result. Where WeChat sign-in is available, we process the authorization code, account identifiers and profile details you authorize. Optional profile details include a nickname and avatar. Authorization is subject to the relevant sign-in screen.

Account IDs, tokens, device identifiers such as Android ID, IP addresses, client version, platform and language support sign-in, request identification and account security. We do not require permission to read your SMS messages to fill in verification codes.

1.2 Maps, location and recording

Location features process coordinates, timestamps, accuracy, altitude, speed and heading. Map and location services may also process authorized network, Wi-Fi or cell information for positioning. Step counts and activity recognition support movement statistics and track quality checks.

These inputs produce routes, endpoints, cities or addresses, activity types, times, distance, duration, pace, ascent and estimated calories. Drift filtering, transport detection and gap compensation may adjust the track and may introduce errors.

Actual location recording and live sharing require location data. Before enabling them, the purpose, necessity, method and impact of sensitive data processing must be explained and separate consent obtained where required. A system location permission does not replace that consent. Refusal affects the relevant feature, without preventing unrelated available features.

1.3 Local records, cloud sync and submissions

Track points and records are stored on your device. After a completed local save, the app currently attempts to create a server summary containing the title, activity type, city or starting address, start and end times and activity statistics. Network failures may leave local and cloud records out of sync.

When you use uploads, sync or route submissions, the server also handles the raw files, map screenshots, waypoints, descriptions and selected images you upload for the corresponding display, route discovery, review and achievement features. Ensure you have permission to import information about others.

1.4 Companion sessions, relationships and discovery

Companion sessions use room and account IDs, membership roles, joining and leaving times, online status, live locations, speed, heading, text messages and key events for communication, member display and session records. Following, follower, collection and route-use records support relationships and route services.

Recommendations may use selected cities, activity types, browsing, collection and usage behavior to select and rank routes. The control for non-personalized recommendations is {非个性化推荐入口}; a working choice must be provided before personalized services are formally offered.

1.5 Feedback, diagnostics and usage analytics

Feedback includes your text, optional contact details and selected images. Diagnostics may contain device, OS and app versions, network status, errors and related actions. Avoid uploading verification codes, tokens or unnecessary location details.

Our own analytics endpoint processes installation and session identifiers, signed-in user IDs where applicable, page views, clicks, recording and upload outcomes, city codes, and device and network information for feature analysis, recommendation evaluation and troubleshooting. Pseudonymized analytics can still be personal information. Event properties have redaction rules for phone numbers, tokens, verification codes, raw coordinates and track points. Recording and companion location data are handled separately as described above. Analytics consent and controls: {使用统计授权及关闭入口}.

3. Track and companion visibility

Local and cloud records have different visibility. Ordinary cloud tracks are currently created in a state eligible for browsing, search or recommendation to other signed-in users. Requiring sign-in does not make a track private. Automatic summary creation, later raw file upload and disclosure must be clearly explained in their respective flows with legally required consent. Publication confirmation and privacy controls: {轨迹公开确认及隐私控制入口}.

Public information may include nicknames, avatars, achievements, following and follower relationships and public tracks. Phone numbers and tokens are not public profile content. Reviewers may inspect route submissions; approved submissions may appear in recommendations or route displays. Withdrawing a submission does not delete the underlying track.

Companion room members can receive your location and session status. Forwarding invitations, codes or QR images may expand access. Hosts and members may retain screenshots or records. Leaving stops future sharing, but does not retract information already seen or saved. Retention of server session records, snapshots and events is addressed below.

Recipients you choose through system sharing or export receive the selected files. Avoid exposing homes, children's activity locations or other people's sensitive information.
